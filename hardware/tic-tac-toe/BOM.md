# Bill of materials

## Printed parts

| Item | Quantity | Suggested material/colour | Approximate sliced use |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Hashtag board | 1 | Black Bambu PLA-CF | 42 g |
| X tokens | 6 | Candy Red Bambu PLA Silk+ | 26.32 g total |
| O tokens | 6 | Titan Gray Bambu PLA Silk+ | 24.88 g total |
| X pickup-zone frame | 1 | Black Bambu PLA-CF | 7.44 g |
| O pickup-zone frame | 1 | Black Bambu PLA-CF | 7.44 g |

The game pieces need no fasteners. Use a removable, non-slip method to keep the
board and pickup-zone frames from moving on the work surface. Any tape, mat or
fixture must remain outside the gripper path and must not change the board height
enough to invalidate the trained reach geometry.

Materials are the validated visual/print configuration, not a requirement for
editing the CAD. If colours or surface finish change, verify the top and wrist
camera segmentation against representative dataset frames before motion.
